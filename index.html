About Development Delays

A practice built on patience, science, and love

👩‍⚕️

Development Delays is led by a licensed clinical therapist dedicated to supporting autistic children and children with developmental delays. We believe every child can grow, connect, and thrive when given the right support at the right pace.

Our sessions are playful, structured, and always centered on your child's strengths. We celebrate every milestone — big or small.

Our Approach

What makes our care different

  • Child-led: Therapy built around your child's interests and pace.
  • Evidence-based: Techniques grounded in current research and best practice.
  • Family-focused: Parents and caregivers are involved at every step.
  • Neurodiversity-affirming: We honor each child's identity while building skills for life.
  • Measurable progress: Clear goals and regular updates you can see.
  • Sensory-friendly: A calm, welcoming environment for every session.
